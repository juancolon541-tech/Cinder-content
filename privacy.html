Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

Cinder Content

Cinder Content is a desktop-based tool used to assist with the creation, management, and publishing of original short-form video content.

Information We Access

When a user connects a TikTok account, Cinder Content may access basic account information and permissions authorized by the user, including permissions necessary to upload video content to the user's TikTok account.

How Information Is Used

Information is used only to provide the features requested by the user, including account authorization and uploading user-approved video content to TikTok.

Data Sharing

Cinder Content does not sell personal information. Information may be transmitted to TikTok when necessary to provide TikTok integration features requested by the user.

Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect account credentials, authorization tokens, and other information used by the service.

User Control

Users may revoke Cinder Content's access to their TikTok account through the applicable TikTok account settings.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be submitted through the Cinder Content project owner.
